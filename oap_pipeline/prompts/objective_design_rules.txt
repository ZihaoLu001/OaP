OBJECTIVE DESIGN RULES (generation, self-review and execution feedback)

Use the same twelve Table I residuals for every task. Keep the original task,
scene observations and controller settings unchanged when revising a program.

1. A terminal states the measurable conditions required to finish a stage.
   All conditions must hold together. Running terms guide motion toward them.
   Predicted satisfaction alone never advances the stage. A gripper command
   describes the acknowledged control input, not proof of a physical grasp.
   Express grasp and release outcomes with object_held and not_held.
2. Running costs use sqrt(1+(r/s)^2)-1; terminal costs use
   (max(r-tau,0)/s)^2. Choose positive scales and physically measurable
   terminal tolerances. Preserve the instruction's target and acceptance
   requirement. Smaller scales increase a term's influence; inspect competing
   terms before tightening a scale.
3. Use supplied body centres, axes, dimensions and relative targets. Allow
   clearance for the fingers. A held object may need to remain above its
   support until release; do not demand incompatible hold and release states.
   In a tool-mediated push, guide the tool toward the target, with appropriate
   contact height and a tool_mediation running term.
4. Boolean held costs have no continuous approach signal. Pair them with
   geometric running terms that guide acquisition. Holding is a finite cost,
   not a hard rollout rejection or an additional stage-transition condition.
5. Every stage declares movable_objects: exactly the objects it intends to
   move. Include grasped/carried objects and a pushed target where appropriate.
   Motion of an omitted object is penalized by C_physics. An unnecessary entry
   removes that protection; [] means no scene object should move.
6. Check that each stage can start from the preceding stage's terminal state.
   Preserve stages that remain consistent with the task and measurements.
   Do not change the task target, introduce unobserved state or treat an
   unexecuted revision as a success.

For the upright Cup task, rotation_progress about its initial material +Y
axis expresses positive tilt toward robot-base +X. Use body="cup",
axis=[0,1,0], min_angle_deg=20, scale_deg=10, and require object_held at
completion. The rotation reference is fixed at episode start. scale_deg=10
matches the 10-degree scale of the other angular terms, so the tilt does not
overpower holding the cup; it is not an allowed reduction of the requested
20-degree minimum.
